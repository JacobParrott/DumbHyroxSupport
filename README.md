# Dumb Hyrox

**Dumb Hyrox** is an intuitive mobile and watch application designed to help you master your fitness routines and track your progress through intensive workout sessions. Using a clean, high-contrast interface, the app allows you to seamlessly edit workouts on your phone, rearrange your exercises, and track your performance live right from your Apple Watch.

**🔒 Privacy Policy for Dumb Hyrox**

Last Updated: May 25, 2026
Dumb Hyrox ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information, particularly regarding health and fitness data accessed via Apple HealthKit.
**1. Data Collection and Usage**
We do not collect personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily provide it to us for customer support purposes.
To provide our core fitness tracking features, Dumb Hyrox requests explicit permission to read and write data to the Apple Health app (HealthKit).
What we access: We only access specific health and workout metrics (such as active energy burned, heart rate, and workouts) that you explicitly authorize.
How we use it: This data is used strictly within the app to calculate your workout routines, track your Hyrox progress, and log your completed sessions.
**2. Strict Protection of HealthKit Data (Apple Guideline 5.1.1 Compliance)**
We recognize that your health data is highly sensitive. In accordance with Apple’s App Store Guidelines, we enforce the following strict limitations on any data accessed via HealthKit:
No Advertising: We will never use your HealthKit data for marketing, advertising, or similar use cases.
No Selling of Data: We will never sell, trade, or rent your HealthKit data to data brokers, advertising platforms, or information resellers.
No Unauthorized Sharing: We will not disclose your HealthKit data to any third party without your express permission, and only if that third party is providing a direct health or fitness service to you.
**3. Data Storage, Retention, and Processing**
Dumb Hyrox is designed with a privacy-first, local architecture.
All custom routines, workout histories, and HealthKit data are processed locally on your device.
If you use iCloud, your data may be securely synced to your private Apple iCloud account. We do not maintain our own external servers to store or analyze your health data.
**4. Data Deletion and Revoking Consent**
Because your health data is stored locally on your device and within the Apple Health app, you have complete control over your information at all times.
To revoke access: You can revoke Dumb Hyrox’s read and write access to your health data at any time by opening the Settings app > Health > Data Access & Devices > Dumb Hyrox, and turning off the permissions.
To delete data: You can delete all data generated by Dumb Hyrox directly within the Apple Health app. Deleting the Dumb Hyrox app from your device will also remove any locally stored app preferences.
**5. Contact Us**
For a detailed look at our privacy practices, or if you have any questions regarding your data, please contact us at parrottdev@icloud.com.
